Five DevOps scenario questions

1. A production deployment doubled API latency. What would you check first?
2. Your Terraform plan wants to replace a critical database. How would you proceed?
3. A pipeline succeeds locally but fails in CI. How would you isolate the difference?
4. Kubernetes pods restart only during peak traffic. What signals would you examine?
5. A secret was committed to Git. What immediate and follow-up actions are required?

Strong answers explain how you gather evidence, reduce risk, communicate and prevent recurrence—not just which command you would run.
